queens: backtracking vs genetic search

Time to a first solution, measured by the solver's own clock. Backtracking is deterministic, so each size is the median of repeated runs. The genetic search is not, and dead-ends often, so each size is several runs: the line is the mean of the wins and the band around it is the fastest to the slowest of them. The cost per solution including the failed runs is in the table below. Genetic search pulls ahead for good at n=28. The cheapest population runs about 2x the board size, or 20x if you want it to succeed most of the time.
